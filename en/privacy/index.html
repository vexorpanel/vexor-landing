Emailgerard@vexorconsulting.com

2. What data we process

This site has no forms. If you message us on WhatsApp, we process your phone number, your profile name and the content of the messages you send us. If you email us, we process your address and the content of the message.

If you accept measurement cookies, we also process aggregated usage data: visits, reaching the results block, language changes and clicks on the contact buttons.

3. What we use it for and the legal basis

  • Handling your enquiry and, if there is interest, preparing a proposal. Legal basis: your consent when you contact us (Art. 6.1.a GDPR) and pre-contractual measures at your request (Art. 6.1.b).
  • Measuring use of the site, only if you accept measurement cookies. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6.1.a).

4. Recipients

We do not share your data with third parties unless legally required. WhatsApp is a Meta service, which processes data under its own privacy policy.

5. How long we keep it

For as long as needed to handle your enquiry and, afterwards, for the legal limitation periods.

6. Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and portability by writing to gerard@vexorconsulting.com. You can also withdraw your consent at any time and lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).
